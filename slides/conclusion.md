## In conclusion

* **Git** is the foundation of modern software engineering and **Infrastructure as Code (`IaC`)**.
* It unlocks **DevOps** and **GitOps** workflows by making every change peer-reviewed, traceable, and reproducible.
* It sits at the heart of **CI/CD pipelines**, automating testing, security scanning, and deployments from every commit.
* Mastering Git lets you apply proven software engineering practices across both **application code** and **cloud infrastructure**.
